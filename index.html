A regular map makes large, sparsely populated counties look important. This one sizes each county by its winning margin instead: the number of extra votes the winning side got there. Counties that were close or have few voters shrink; populous counties won by a wide margin grow large. Green counties voted Yes, red counties voted No. Use the tabs on top of the map to switch between four views:

  • Real map: Colorado as you know it, each county colored by the side that won it.
  • Margin map: the same counties resized by their winning margin, kept near their real locations.
  • By winner: counties sorted to a Yes side and a No side, split by each side’s share of the statewide vote. Each side lists how many counties it won and their combined margin; the difference between the two totals is the statewide margin.
  • Pie chart: the counties melt into the statewide result, the Yes and No shares of every vote cast.
